# Evaluation

This page records how Allelion Verdict AI was measured, what it scored, and where it fails. Every number here is generated by `make evaluate`, `make evaluate_blind` or `make postmortem` and stored in `reports/evaluation_summary.json`, `reports/blind_postmortem.json` and the prediction tables beside them.

## Protocol

The reference standard is the classification ClinVar shows for variants reviewed by an expert panel or covered by a practice guideline (review status of three or four stars). These are the only ClinVar records where a named group such as ENIGMA, InSiGHT or a ClinGen variant curation expert panel has applied the ACMG/AMP framework with case data, functional assays and multifactorial likelihood models. Every such variant in the knowledge base went into the blind set: 8,733 variants in 17 genes.

The blind set was frozen and hashed on 16 October 2026 at 17:10:27 UTC, before any evidence table, predictor, rule threshold or retrieval index existed. Its SHA256 is `136ffc28425ec473c349e9e1e5d210e2df5d31d821412c022e5cecc7b5adf9cd`, recorded in `data/eval/blind_hash.txt`. The evaluation script refuses to run if the hash no longer matches, and appends a timestamp to `data/eval/blind_runs.json` every time the blind set is scored. That file holds a single entry: 18:52:16 UTC on 16 October 2026. The blind set was scored once, after every component was frozen, and nothing was changed afterwards.

Every choice before that run used the development set: 3,300 two star variants from 1,112 genes, stratified to 700 per class (500 likely pathogenic, the number available after stratification). The remaining 269,419 variants form the evidence pool, from which allelic evidence (PS1, PM5), hot spot density (PM1), gene spectra (PP2, BP1) and the training data for the predictors are drawn. Each variant is excluded from its own evidence: the engine never sees the classification it is being compared with.

The question sets follow the same pattern: 60 development questions for tuning retrieval and the writer, and 30 blind questions frozen with their own hash (`data/eval/questions_blind_hash.txt`) and scored in the same single run.

## Metrics

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Exact five tier | predicted class equals the reference class |
| Within one tier | predicted class is the reference or an adjacent class |
| Three tier | agreement after merging P with LP and B with LB |
| Balanced three tier | mean recall over P/LP, VUS and B/LB, so the large pathogenic share in the blind set does not dominate |
| Severe discordance | a pathogenic or likely pathogenic reference called benign or likely benign, or the reverse; the error that changes clinical management |
| Definitive call rate | share of variants given a P, LP, LB or B call rather than VUS |
| P/LP precision | of the variants the engine calls pathogenic or likely pathogenic, the share the panel agrees are pathogenic or likely pathogenic |

## Blind results

| Method | Exact five tier | Within one tier | Three tier | Balanced three tier | Severe discordance | P/LP precision | B/LB precision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Point system (primary) | 17.7% | 70.9% | 61.2% | 63.6% | 68 (0.78%) | 99.8% | 84.4% |
| 2015 combining rules | 10.0% | 29.8% | 12.5% | 35.1% | 1 (0.01%) | 96.2% | 86.0% |
| Learned verdict | 69.2% | 88.4% | 83.1% | 75.0% | 573 (6.56%) | 87.6% | 91.1% |

The point system is the product's classification. It follows the Bayesian point scale of Tavtigian and colleagues (2020) with the ClinGen Sequence Variant Interpretation refinements. Of 3,572 variants it called pathogenic or likely pathogenic, 3,566 were pathogenic or likely pathogenic by the panel: a precision of 99.8%. It reached that precision without any of the case level evidence (segregation, de novo status, functional assays, phenotype specificity, allelic data from probands) that the panels used, which is why exact agreement is low: 3,443 of 4,868 pathogenic variants were called likely pathogenic, one tier short, because PVS1 plus PM2 at supporting strength reaches nine points rather than ten.

The 2015 combining rules are scored on the same criteria as a baseline. Because PM2 is now applied at supporting strength, PVS1 plus PM2 no longer satisfies any 2015 likely pathogenic rule, so almost every pathogenic variant falls to VUS. This is the motivation for the point system, and the gap on the blind set (12.5% against 61.2% three tier agreement) is the measured size of that effect.

The learned verdict is a LightGBM multiclass model over the criteria, points and raw features, trained on the evidence pool with gene grouped cross validation. It agrees with the panels far more often (83.1% three tier) but makes 573 severe errors, 438 of them benign variants called pathogenic. It is shown in the interface as a second opinion and never replaces the criteria based call.

PVS1 carries the blind set, as expected for two tumour suppressor genes dominated by truncating variants. Computational evidence adds nine percentage points of agreement but is also where most severe errors come from (removing it cuts the severe rate from 0.78% to 0.09%). Gene level criteria make no net difference on these genes, and removing BP1 slightly improves balanced agreement because the mismatch repair genes and BRCA2 do carry pathogenic missense variants outside their curated hot spots.

### Posterior reliability

The point total maps to a posterior probability of pathogenicity under the Tavtigian model (prior 0.10, OddsPath 350 for very strong). On the blind set the posterior is well ordered:

| Posterior band | Variants | Reference P/LP share |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 0.001 | 97 | 0.0% |
| 0.001 to 0.1 | 1,276 | 5.3% |
| 0.1 to 0.5 | 3,520 | 34.5% |
| 0.5 to 0.9 | 299 | 85.6% |
| 0.9 to 0.99 | 3,493 | 99.9% |
| 0.99 to 1 | 48 | 97.9% |

The 0.1 to 0.5 band is the band of variants the engine leaves as uncertain; one third of them are pathogenic by the panel, which is the case data gap described above.

## Post hoc analysis of the blind run

`make postmortem` slices the stored blind predictions. It does not score anything again, and none of the fixes it points to have been applied to the reported numbers.

**Transcript choice explains 59 of the 68 severe errors.** ClinVar records 1,327 of the blind BRCA1 variants on the exon 11 skipping isoforms NM_007298.3 and NM_007299.3. A frameshift inside exon 11 of the canonical transcript NM_007294.3 is then written as a change hundreds of bases into an intron of the short isoform (for example `NM_007298.3:c.788-464delA`, which is `NM_007294.3:c.4035delA`, p.Glu1346LysfsTer). The engine interprets the transcript ClinVar reports, so it sees a deep intronic variant, withholds PVS1 and, when the splice predictor is quiet, applies BP7. Of the 1,002 such variants whose ClinVar record also lists a truncating effect on NM_007294.3, all 1,002 are pathogenic by ENIGMA; the engine called 932 of them VUS and 59 likely benign.

| Slice | Variants | Three tier | Severe | Severe rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Whole blind set | 8,733 | 61.2% | 68 | 0.78% |
| BRCA1 on an exon 11 skipping isoform | 1,327 | 15.0% | 59 | 4.45% |
| Everything else | 7,406 | 69.5% | 9 | 0.12% |
| ENIGMA outside the isoform slice | 4,704 | 67.7% | 2 | 0.04% |

The fix is to evaluate every transcript listed in the record and interpret the variant on the MANE Select or canonical transcript, which requires exon models for transcripts ClinVar does not use as the display transcript. It is the first item on the roadmap below.

**The remaining nine severe errors** are two BRCA2 missense, two PMS2 missense and one MSH6 missense variant that the panels classified as pathogenic or likely pathogenic with functional and multifactorial evidence, while the engine applied BP1 (missense in a gene where truncation is the main mechanism) and nothing else; one MSH2 intronic variant (`c.942+3A>T`, a known founder splice variant) where the frequency in gnomAD triggered BS1; the CFTR p.Phe508del deletion, where BS1 fired because the carrier frequency of a recessive founder allele exceeds the recessive threshold; SHOC2 c.4A>G (p.Ser2Gly), a recurrent gain of function variant whose effect the missense predictor misses, so BP4 applied; and one HRAS variant that ClinVar records as missense on a non canonical transcript while it is synonymous on NM_005343.3, called likely pathogenic through PM5 at strong strength.

**Benign variants are under called rather than mis called.** Of 2,884 benign or likely benign reference variants, 1,724 were left as VUS, almost all intronic or synonymous variants in BRCA1, BRCA2 and the mismatch repair genes that are rare in gnomAD. The panels classify these with multifactorial likelihood ratios and splicing assays the engine does not have; the engine gives them BP7 (one benign point) and stops at likely benign or VUS.

## Development results

| Method | Exact five tier | Within one tier | Three tier | Balanced three tier | Severe |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Point system, tuned rules | 46.6% | 90.9% | 72.9% | 70.8% | 12 (0.36%) |
| Point system, default rules | 46.1% | not stored | 72.2% | 69.8% | 14 (0.42%) |
| 2015 combining rules | 41.4% | 79.2% | 55.8% | 59.7% | 2 (0.06%) |
| Learned verdict | 65.1% | 96.6% | 90.2% | 88.1% | 30 (0.91%) |

Rule tuning searched 648 configurations of seven thresholds (BS1 dominant and recessive frequency, PM1 window and minimum, PP2 minimum and benign fraction, BS2 homozygote count) on the development set only. The selected configuration moved balanced three tier agreement from 69.8% to 70.8% and reduced severe errors.

| Component | Measure | Result |

| Variant mention extractor (CRF) | exact span F1, SETH test documents | 0.878 (regular expressions 0.663) |
| Claim verifier | AUROC on 6,274 held out perturbed sentences | 0.992, accuracy 96.5% with hard rules |
| Reranker | validation nDCG@10 on 599 synthetic queries | 0.991 |

The missense and splice predictors are calibrated to evidence strengths with the local posterior method of Pejaver and colleagues (2022), using a prior of 0.0441 and bootstrap lower bounds. On the missense model this gives PP3 at supporting strength from a score of 0.671 and moderate from 0.803, and BP4 at supporting strength below 0.184 and moderate below 0.073. Neither model reaches strong evidence for missense, which matches published calibrations of single predictors.

The verifier is weakest on argument swaps (53.5% of sentences that swap two entities are caught), the case where every word in the sentence also appears in the evidence. Because the writer is extractive by default, this case does not arise in normal use; it matters when the optional generative writer is enabled.

Across all 90 questions the copilot wrote 315 sentences. Median response time was 292 ms. All five safety probes (crisis language, patient identifiers, an off topic request, a personal medical decision and a prompt injection) were handled as designed.

## Limitations

1. **Transcript choice.** The engine interprets the transcript ClinVar displays. For BRCA1 this misreads exon 11 truncating variants as deep intronic variants. See the post hoc analysis.
2. **No case level data in the benchmark.** PS2, PM6, PS4, PP1, BS4, PM3, PS3 and BS3 are implemented and exercised in the interface, but the benchmark has no case data, so they were never applied. The panels had this evidence, which caps exact agreement.
3. **Circularity in the pool.** Allelic evidence and the predictors learn from ClinVar classifications of other variants. Self exclusion prevents a variant from supporting itself, but classifications made by the same panels on neighbouring variants still inform PM5, PS1 and PM1. Gene grouped cross validation bounds this for the predictors; it cannot be removed from allelic criteria, which are defined in terms of prior classifications.
4. **Panel concentration.** 69% of the blind set comes from ENIGMA and 24% from InSiGHT. Results on 17 genes do not establish performance across the 5,561 genes in the knowledge base. The development set, drawn from 1,112 genes, is the better guide for breadth but its references are less rigorous.
5. **Data age.** The ClinVar and gnomAD join is a 2018 snapshot on GRCh37, chosen because it is the largest public join that can be retrieved reproducibly from pinned commits. Classifications and frequencies have changed since.
6. **Missing structural evidence.** There is no copy number, structural variant, mitochondrial or repeat expansion support, no protein structure, and no conservation scores (PhyloP, GERP) or splice predictions from SpliceAI scores, since those resources could not be retrieved in the build environment.
7. **The learned verdict is not safe on its own.** Its 6.56% severe error rate on the blind set is eight times that of the point system.

## Roadmap

1. Interpret variants on the MANE Select transcript, building exon models for every transcript in the record and choosing the most severe consequence when the display transcript is not canonical.
2. Gene specific rule sets from ClinGen variant curation expert panels (for example the ENIGMA BRCA1 and BRCA2 specifications and the InSiGHT mismatch repair specifications), so BP1 and BS1 follow the panel's own thresholds.
3. A refreshed ClinVar and gnomAD v4 join on GRCh38, and a second blind set frozen from expert panel classifications made after the current snapshot.
